I study how the structure of a learning activity shapes understanding, participation, and knowledge construction. My work on external representations examines how diagrams and tables influence the relationships learners see among concepts. In collaborative learning, I combine social network analysis with close analysis of discussion posts to examine how students take up assigned roles and develop ideas together. I also study how accurately interpretable models can identify these processes in text. A related line of research uses institutional data to examine students’ use of academic support over time. I am now developing a line of research on how learners interpret AI-generated representations of unfamiliar concepts.
