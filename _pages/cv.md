## Research Profile

My research examines how the design and structure of learning environments shape understanding, participation, and knowledge construction. My work connects external representations, collaborative interaction, and instructional design with social network analysis, discourse/content analysis, mixed methods, and interpretable computational approaches. Building on this foundation, I am developing a research agenda on AI-supported learning, with particular interest in AI-generated representations and learner–AI interaction.

**Research areas:** Instructional design and learning experience design; external representations and knowledge structures; collaborative learning and knowledge construction; online and technology-mediated learning; learning analytics; AI-supported learning

**Methods:** Mixed-methods research; program evaluation; thematic and qualitative coding; discourse analysis; social network analysis; survey and instrument design; statistical modeling; interpretable machine learning; NLP/text analysis

**Tools:** R, Python, SPSS, NVivo, Qualtrics, Power BI, Excel, Canvas, Articulate 360
